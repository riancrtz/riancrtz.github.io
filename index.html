2.9:1 → 5.2:1BUTTON TEXT CONTRAST

A design decision I tested, not guessed. White text on the terracotta buttons looked right but measured only 2.9:1, below the 4.5:1 accessibility minimum. I switched it to dark ink, which reaches 5.2:1. Star ratings also show the number beside them, because amber alone is too light to carry meaning.
